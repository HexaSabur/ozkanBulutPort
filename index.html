Meet Ozkan Bulut

Ozkan Bulut is a highly skilled plasterer and renderer based in Adelaide, South Australia, with over 25 years of experience delivering exceptional results across residential and commercial projects.

Specialising in bespoke wall systems, liquid plaster applications and premium renders, Ozkan has built a reputation throughout Adelaide for meticulous attention to detail and an uncompromising commitment to quality.

Whether it's a custom feature wall in a luxury home in Burnside, a full exterior render in Glenelg, or a liquid plaster finish in a contemporary apartment — every project receives the same level of care and precision.
